# ARDS assessment standards

Score eight dimensions from 0 to 4: 0=no demonstrated validity, 1=major gaps, 2=partial/incomplete, 3=correct and adequately justified, 4=independent rigorous demonstration. Use NA if not observable.

1. Mathematical modeling: prose into variables, constraints, formulas.
2. Algorithm discovery: independently find a valid approach.
3. Correctness reasoning: justify all legal inputs.
4. Edge cases and constraints: boundaries, impossibilities, overflow, necessary/sufficient.
5. Complexity reasoning: accurate time and auxiliary-space bounds.
6. Optimization: efficient algorithm with justified bounds.
7. Transfer and generalization: apply insight to unfamiliar forms.
8. Metacognitive calibration: pre-feedback confidence and sound uncertainty analysis.

Keep objective data distinct: first attempt correct? assisted correct? self-reported or recorded time? hint level 0 none, 1 attention cue, 2 conceptual, 3 structural, 4 full solution; verified optimality when supported. Confidence 0-100 must be given **before feedback**.

Initial baseline: 10 problems; weekly: ideally 5 controlled comparable problems; monthly review: distributions, delayed transfer/retention, confidence calibration, and time/accuracy by matched difficulty, with sample sizes. Every 20-30 randomized problems inspect topical coverage and story/abstract proportions. Never fabricate data, and never convert rubric scores into IQ estimates.

Use `templates/progress.csv` as field schema. Preserve independent first attempts even after coached revisions. Unobserved dimension values are blank/NA, not zero.
